Spelling Bazaar

Dzongkha Spelling Learning Game

Developed by: Thinley Wangchuk

Created for: Class 9 Students

Purpose: This game helps students learn correct Dzongkha spellings and vocabularies through fun and interactive gameplay.

Designed to make learning Dzongkha spelling enjoyable for students while improving their language skills.

🇧🇹

The Young Trader's Dream

"In the bustling bazaar of Bhutan, a young boy dreams of becoming the greatest trader in the kingdom.

To succeed, he must master the correct Dzongkha spellings and words".

Join him on this fun learning journey through the Spelling Bazaar!
